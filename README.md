# Superstore Sales Dashboard – Excel Data Analysis

## Project Overview

An interactive Excel dashboard built using the Superstore retail sales dataset to analyze sales, profit, orders, products, regions, categories, segments, shipping modes, and discounts.

The project transforms raw retail data into meaningful business insights using Microsoft Excel.

## Key Business Insights

1. The business generated **$2.30M in sales** and **$286.40K in profit**, resulting in a **12.47% profit margin**.

2. **Standard Class** is the dominant shipping mode, contributing the highest sales and profit.

3. Higher discount levels are generally associated with lower profitability, with **discounts of 30%+ frequently resulting in losses**.

4. **Technology performs strongly in the West and East regions**, while Furniture in the Central region is underperforming.

5. Sales and profit fluctuate throughout the year, with **December 2014 showing particularly strong performance**, while July 2014 recorded negative profit.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Review high-discount orders to reduce margin losses.
- Investigate underperforming Furniture performance in the Central region.
- Focus on high-performing products and categories.
- Evaluate shipping strategies to optimize profitability.
- Monitor monthly sales trends to identify seasonal opportunities.
